Wallahi I Fumbled

Paste your Solana wallet. We replay every memecoin you sold and show how much SOL you'd have if you'd sold at the top instead.

We check your biggest sells first, since that's where the worst fumbles live. Takes 5 to 10 seconds.

How the numbers work
  • Fumbled: for each token you sold, (all-time high price − your average sell price) × tokens sold, converted to SOL at today's price.
  • If you'd held: same math using today's price instead of the all-time high. For most memecoins this is near zero, because they died.
  • Skipped: tokens you never sold, sells under $1, tokens with no recorded all-time high. Only sells paid out in SOL are counted.
  • Data from Solana Tracker. For entertainment, not financial advice.
